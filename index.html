How to score 1 minute read
  1. Score each team straight after their demo, on three areas, 1 to 5 each. That's 15 from you, 45 per team across the three judges.
  2. Wow factor: something surprising, innovative or magical, in the product itself or in how it was shown.
  3. Functionality: how well it solves the product owner's problem statement. Score what you saw working, not what was promised.
  4. Presentation: 10 minutes for pitch and demo. A joint delivery by the product owner and developers scores higher; if only one side presented, give 2 at most. Tick presented jointly when it felt built together.
  5. Use the whole scale and keep 5 for the truly outstanding:
    1Weak
    2Below par
    3Solid
    4Strong
    5Outstanding
    Tap a number to see what it means for that area.
  6. Scores save as you tap and only you can change yours. Jot a note on each team: it helps when the judges compare. Ties go to the higher Functionality, then Wow factor.

Each judge scores three areas out of 5 (max 15 per judge, 45 per team). Ties are ranked by Functionality, then Wow factor. "Joint" counts the judges who confirmed the product owner and developers presented together. Refreshes every 5 seconds.
